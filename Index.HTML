Farm details

1 · Farm shape

Or pick Boundary below and tap the map to place corners of any shape. Tap the first corner (or Close) to finish. Edit plants mode: tap a plant of the selected species to remove it, tap empty ground to add one.

Farm size & boundary

Draw the map first, then enter the real area (or one real edge length). The whole shape scales to match. Spacings stay in real metres.

Edit corner coordinates (m)

2 · Models from your deck

Zones are % of the farm's width/height, so a model rescales to any area or shape. Every species stays editable below.

3 · Species, spacing & zones

Tap a species card to select it. Use Block mode to draw a separate area for the selected species; otherwise it fills the whole farm.

4 · Tools

If download doesn't start, long-press (or right-click) the image and choose Save image.
